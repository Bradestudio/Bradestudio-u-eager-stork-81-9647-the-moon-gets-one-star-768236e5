## Entry (116 characters)

> One star. The Moon never answers its texts, shows up late every night, and still expects us to plan tides around it.

## Why it works

A real review shape: rating first ("One star."), then a concrete grievance (never answers its texts, always late), then the kicker — the Moon still expects us to plan tides around it. The Moon as a flaky roommate who runs the tides: relatable, specific, and fresh, not a stock Moon joke.

## How it works
